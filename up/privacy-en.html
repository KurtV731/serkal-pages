Privacy Policy

This page explains in plain language which data the website and SerKal 1.2.4 for Windows and Android process, why Google and TMDB are used, and how you can delete data.

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · SerKal 1.2.4
SummaryControllerWebsiteSerKal 1.2.4GoogleTMDBStorage & deletionYour rights

1. Summary

In brief: SerKal does not operate a central SerKal user account or its own online database containing your series. Desktop and Android process your series archive locally. If you enable the Google features, the complete archive is stored in the SerKal-specific, normally hidden app-data area of your Google Drive. After changes, the complete archive is written back when the app closes normally. This is not continuous device synchronisation.
  • Google associates the archive with your selected account and enables calendar entries initiated by you.
  • TMDB supplies search results, series information, images and provider information.
  • The website is hosted by ALL-INKL.COM. SerKal currently has no built-in advertising, analytics or payment services.
  • Your own notes remain part of your archive; when using the calendar feature, selected SerKal content may also appear in the calendar event.

Desktop and Android

Both applications download the archive from the Google Drive app-data area, process it locally and, after changes, upload the complete state when the app closes normally. Android can also write the archive. There is no continuous real-time or background synchronisation between open devices; concurrent editing can therefore produce competing states.

The Desktop app stores settings and required Google access tokens as local application files. Android obtains Google access tokens via Google sign-in for the relevant session, retains limited account and display data locally, and protects the locally cached TMDB access credential using Android Keystore. SerKal does not store Google passwords.

6. TMDB and provider information

When you search, SerKal sends the search text, language, country and, where applicable, series or season identifiers and the technical API credential to The Movie Database (TMDB). TMDB supplies titles, descriptions, images, release dates and – where available – provider information based on JustWatch data. TMDB also processes standard connection data such as your IP address when content is requested. Your own notes are not sent to TMDB.

More information: TMDB Privacy Policy. SerKal is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

7. Purposes, legal bases and transfers

App functions initiated by you are processed to provide SerKal (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Linking your Google account and the permissions granted for it are based on your deliberate authorisation, which you may revoke at Google at any time. Website security and error analysis are based on our legitimate interest in a secure, functional service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Google and TMDB may also process data outside the EU or EEA. Their respective terms and statutory transfer mechanisms apply to that processing. SerKal does not disclose your archive for advertising purposes and does not sell personal data.

8. Storage and deletion

Local data

Local settings, caches, access information and any local archive copies can be removed by deleting the SerKal app data or SerKal application folder. Uninstalling alone does not reliably remove all local files on every system.

Google Drive app data

Removing the app or revoking Google access stops future access, but does not automatically delete data already stored in the Drive app-data area. You can remove it in Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps → SerKal → Options → Delete hidden app data, where Google provides that option for the connection. Alternatively, contact support@serkal.de; we can only assist with steps for which you are signed in and authorised.

Google Calendar

Calendar events are separate from the archive. Delete unwanted SerKal events or the calendar created by SerKal directly in Google Calendar. Deleting a series in SerKal may remove the episode events managed by SerKal when the app closes normally; for complete control, also review and delete the entries in Google Calendar.

Important: Local data, Drive app data and calendar data are held in different places and must be handled separately for complete deletion.

9. Security

Connections to the website, Google and TMDB use encrypted HTTPS connections. Android protects the locally cached TMDB credential using Android Keystore; other local data relies on the protection of your device and user account. SerKal does not add separate end-to-end encryption to the series archive. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security.
